Rev. Isaac Opoku Agyemang

General Overseer & Spiritual Father
"You are never alone. In God's presence, there is fullness of joy, healing, and an unshakeable peace that surpasses all human understanding."

Rev. Isaac Opoku Agyemang is a devoted teacher of the Gospel, visionary leader, and pastor with a heart for soul-winning and kingdom impact. Through prophetic prayer, sound biblical doctrine, and pastoral counseling, he has ministered hope and direction to hundreds of families across Accra and beyond.
