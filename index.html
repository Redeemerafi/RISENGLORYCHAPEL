Who We Are

A Sanctuary of Hope & Spiritual Awakening

At Risen Glory Chapel, our heartbeat is to build people of purpose, foster unshakeable faith in Jesus Christ, and share God's unconditional love across our community.

Prophetic Prayer & Revival

Experiencing the power of targeted prayer, spiritual deliverance, and supernatural restoration through the Holy Spirit.

Biblical Truth & Growth

Grounding believers in the uncompromised Word of God with practical wisdom for everyday living, business, and family.

Loving Care & Outreach

Demonstrating Christ's compassion through active charity missions, including our annual Widowhood Support Program.

Rev. Isaac Opoku Agyemang

General Overseer & Spiritual Father
"You are never alone. In God's presence, there is fullness of joy, healing, and an unshakeable peace that surpasses all human understanding."

Rev. Isaac Opoku Agyemang is a devoted teacher of the Gospel, visionary leader, and pastor with a heart for soul-winning and kingdom impact. Through prophetic prayer, sound biblical doctrine, and pastoral counseling, he ministers hope and divine direction to families across Accra and beyond.
